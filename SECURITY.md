# Security

## Reporting a vulnerability

- **In the libreSRS software:** write to security@libresrs.org. Please do not open a public issue
  for it.
- **On a running instance:** write to that instance's security contact. It is in the instance's
  `/.well-known/security.txt` and in its Privacy Policy (`/privacy#security`).

Test against your own copy of libreSRS, not against another operator's students. Do not access,
keep or share anyone's data.

## What a report means for student data

Each instance's operator is responsible for its own privacy policy, including how it treats a
report that touched student data and whom it notifies; read it at `/privacy` on that instance.

Operators set their own contact and pages: see `doc/legal-documents.md`.
