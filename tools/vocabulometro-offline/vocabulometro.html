Panel del profesor · progreso de la clase

Method. Items are stratified by corpus frequency; band token mass comes from the lexicon-filtered OpenSubtitles-2018 Spanish list (top 500 = 70.2% of tokens; top 5,000 = 88.0%). Sí/No scores are corrected for overclaiming with the pseudoword false-alarm rate, k̂ = (h − f)/(1 − f); size = Σ Nᵦk̂ᵦ and coverage = Σ Mᵦk̂ᵦ. 95% CIs use Agresti–Coull-stabilized band variances plus the shared false-alarm term [Σwᵦ(1−hᵦ)]²·Var(f̂)/(1−f̂)⁴, validated by simulation to ≥95% actual coverage. Top-500 mastery uses 4-option meaning items with formula scoring (wrong answers penalized ⅓; "No lo sé" is a neutral omit). CEFR estimates use X_Lex size bands (Milton & Alexiou 2009) as a lexical proxy with CI-aware level ranges. Recognition ≠ depth: treat results as a coverage audit of the input diet, not a grade.
